Real-Time Front-End Adaptation for Energy-Efficient mmWave Radar Sensor

Jinhyeok Park, Sudarshan Sharma, Wei-Chun Wang, Alex Saad-Falcon, Russ H. Huang, Alyosha Molnar, Justin Romberg, Saibal Mukhopadhyay
IEEE SENSORS, 2025
PDF

Abstract

Radar sensor systems often suffer from suboptimal performance in dynamic environments due to static front-end configurations. While cognitive radar approaches have been proposed to address this issue through adaptive processing, they are often computationally intensive and unsuitable for real-time deployment. This work proposed a lightweight, feedback-driven adaptation framework on a frame-by-frame basis. Target information is extracted from the Range-Doppler map and detection accuracy is calculated by range errors between predicted and detected values. A pre-trained machine learning (ML) model adapts the front-end with low latency, while risk levels of errors are assessed using predefined policies. Proposed work was validated on an AWR1843 mmWave radar sensor, achieves a 2.7× improvement in energy efficiency while satisfying the latency constraints.
