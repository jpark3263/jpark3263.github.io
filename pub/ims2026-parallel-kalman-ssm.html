Parallel Kalman Filtering with Physics-Informed Selective State-Space Models for Robust Radar Sensing

Jinhyeok Park, Apurba Prasad Padhy, Han Cho, Saibal Mukhopadhyay
IEEE MTT-S Radio Frequency Systems and Applications Symposium (IMS RFSA), 2026, pp. 63–66
PDF

Abstract

Classical Kalman filters struggle under nonlinear dynamics, while neural-network-assisted Kalman filters improve robustness but still rely on strict frame-by-frame updates. However, real-world radar sensing frequently violates this assumption due to synchronization errors, clutter, sensor dropouts, and hardware latency. To address these challenges, we propose a physics-informed long-sequence Kalman filtering framework that leverages state-space neural architectures to model extended temporal structure beyond the limits of frame-by-frame filtering. By incorporating known system and observation matrices, the model learns an adaptive Kalman gain with substantially reduced training requirements. Evaluated on the RADIATE dataset, the proposed method achieves 5.5 dB higher accuracy under clean conditions, delivers more than 9.5 dB improvement under frame-drop scenarios, and provides 2.5× faster sequence-level inference than neural-network-assisted Kalman filters.
